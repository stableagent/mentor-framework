# Output Projection Standard

All meeting documents are views over one canonical Meeting Record.

## Required projections

1. Executive Summary
2. Detailed Minutes
3. Decision Log
4. Action Register
5. Risk Register
6. Assumption Register
7. Open Question Register
8. Disagreement Log
9. Evidence Register
10. Specialist Contribution Summary
11. Follow-up Agenda
12. Final Advisory Report

Each projection references the same meeting ID and underlying entity IDs.

## Integrity

A projection must never invent a decision, owner, due date, source, participant, specialist approval, or consensus. Unknown values remain unknown.
